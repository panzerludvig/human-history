## 03 — Superseded models out of the game build

Four commits, `98515c4` to `38649f9`. Six probe files and scripts beyond the Files list went with the headers they tested.

**Probe.** `build\sweep.exe earth 0 rules 1` identical to the baseline (`fc`: no differences); none of the four headers in `main.cpp`'s includes; every `build_*.bat` exits 0 with no new warning.

**Unsure of.** The 2026-09-07 entry in [[Meta/Open Threads]] still names `qg2.h` as a dated record. The probe scripts other than `build.bat` do not create `build\`.

**Verdict:** keep. The per-model calls are right: QG2GEO is the atmosphere being redone on the mesh; the rest is recorded in the branch map at `4ac16a0`.
